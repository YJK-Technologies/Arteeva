What You Receive

What each division delivers to you: consulting schedules, cost forecasts, coordination trackers, and reports, or completed construction works.

DIVISION DELIVERABLES

From Arteeva Consulting & Arteeva Construction

Arteeva Consulting
Planning & Scheduling

Baseline Schedule & Programme Narrative

The plan, its logic, and the assumptions behind it. Includes work breakdown, baseline schedule development, critical path logic, and realistic timelines from brief to handover.

Arteeva Consulting
Design & Procurement

Design & Procurement Deliverables Tracker

What is still needed, from whom, and by when. Coordinates drawing and submittal tracking, RFI and approval follow-up, material approvals, and long-lead item monitoring.

Arteeva Consulting
Delay & Time Analysis

Delay or Time Impact Analysis Report

The cause of delay and its effect on completion. Critical path and time impact analysis providing the technical schedule basis for Extension of Time submissions where appropriate.

Arteeva Construction
Select Building Works

Residential & Commercial Buildings

Completed residential and commercial buildings, built to the agreed scope, specification, programme and budget in writing by our site team and specialist subcontractors.

Arteeva Construction
Civil, Fit-Out & Handover

Civil Works, Renovation & Fit-Out

Completed site and civil works, plus renovated and fitted-out spaces carried out to the agreed scope, with quality-checked stages, a safely managed site, and handover documentation.

Arteeva Consulting
Cost Management & Control

Project Budget & Cash-Flow Forecast

What the project is expected to cost, and when. Covers budget setup support, cost tracking, cash-flow forecasting, and transparent cost variance reporting.

Arteeva Consulting
Project Controls & Reporting

Monthly Progress, Cost & Variance Report

Where the project stands against plan. Combines progress monitoring, schedule updates, and earned value where it suits the project into short, decision-focused management reports.

Arteeva Construction
Select Industrial Works

Industrial & Warehouse Works

Completed warehouses, sheds, and industrial buildings built to the agreed scope and specification, tracked against a written plan with recorded change control.

Arteeva Consulting
Change, Risk & Dashboards

Change Log, Lookahead & Dashboard

What has changed and its effect on time and cost, alongside lookahead schedules and project dashboards showing what must happen next and the project at a glance.

Note on Illustrations: Illustrations and imagery on this site are for explanation only and do not depict client projects. Arteeva keeps its consultancy and construction work separate.

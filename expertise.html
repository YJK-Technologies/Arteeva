HOW WE WORK

Logic-Based Planning, Consistent Measurement & Transparent Reporting

Arteeva has two divisions of equal focus, sharing one discipline: plan the work, measure it, and report it clearly. Arteeva Consulting provides project management consultancy centred on planning, scheduling, cost management, coordination and project controls—helping clients set realistic baselines for time and cost, keep design and procurement deliverables moving, and understand where a project stands and why.

Arteeva Construction executes select small-scale construction works, built with our own site team and specialist subcontractors, and managed to the same planning, cost and progress discipline. We work alongside clients' design teams, suppliers and contractors, and we run the two divisions separately so that our consulting advice stays independent.

Independent

OUR DOMAINS

Consulting & Construction Disciplines

Core project management consultancy capabilities and select construction execution disciplines across both divisions.

Planning and Scheduling

Work breakdown, baseline schedule development, logic and critical path, and lookahead planning. We deliver a baseline schedule and programme narrative setting out the plan, its logic, and the assumptions behind it.

  • Work Breakdown & Baseline Schedule
  • Logic, Critical Path & Narrative
  • Short-Term Lookahead Planning

Cost Management and Control

Budget setup support, cost tracking, cash-flow forecasting, variance reporting, and change and variation tracking. We help you see clearly what the project is expected to cost, and when.

  • Project Budget Setup & Tracking
  • Cash-Flow Forecasting & Variance
  • Change and Variation Log

Design & Procurement Coordination

Facilitating and coordinating design and procurement deliverables between the client, design team, suppliers and site so that decisions and deliverables arrive when the programme needs them.

  • Drawing, Submittal & RFI Tracking
  • Material Approval Follow-Up
  • Procurement & Long-Lead Monitoring

Project Controls and Reporting

Progress monitoring and schedule updates, earned value where it suits the project, dashboards and management reports. Reports are short, readable, and aimed at decisions.

  • Progress Monitoring & Updates
  • Earned Value & Project Dashboards
  • Monthly Progress, Cost & Variance Report

Delay, Time and Change Analysis

Critical path and time impact analysis, with the technical schedule basis for Extension of Time submissions where appropriate. We deliver clear reports on the cause of delay and its effect on completion.

  • Critical Path & Time Impact Analysis
  • EOT Technical Schedule Basis
  • Change Effect on Cost & Time

Risk & Project Management Support

Early identification of slippage, cost pressure and critical-path constraints, combined with coordination between project parties, governance, meeting follow-up, and decision and action tracking.

  • Early Slippage & Constraint Warning
  • Project Party Coordination & Governance
  • Decision, Action & Stakeholder Tracking

Buildings & Industrial Works

Executed by Arteeva Construction: new residential and commercial buildings, warehouses, sheds and industrial buildings built to the agreed scope, programme and budget in writing.

  • Residential & Commercial Buildings
  • Warehouses, Sheds & Industrial Works
  • Tracked Against Agreed Written Plan

Civil Works, Renovation & Fit-Out

Site development and civil works, alongside renovation, refurbishment and interior fit-out of existing spaces—carried out by our own site team and labour with coordinated specialist subcontractors.

  • Site Development & Civil Works
  • Renovation, Refurbishment & Fit-Out
  • Own Site Team & Specialist Trades

Quality, Safety and Handover

We take on a limited number of construction works at a time so each project receives close management attention—with quality checks at key stages, agreed site safety arrangements, and handover documentation.

  • Stage Quality Checks & Records Kept
  • Pre-Agreed Site Safety Arrangements
  • Organised Handover Documentation

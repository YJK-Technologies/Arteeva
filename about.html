WHO WE ARE

A Heritage of Structural Integrity & Engineering Leadership

ARTEEVA was established to solve one of the construction sector’s greatest challenges: bridging the critical gap between conceptual architectural vision and rigorous on-the-ground engineering execution.

As a full-spectrum general contracting and civil engineering firm, ARTEEVA has delivered dozens of iconic commercial headquarters, industrial manufacturing complexes, modern logistics facilities, and exclusive luxury residential residences. We pride ourselves on operating with total accountability—ensuring structural safety, fiscal transparency, and milestone accuracy for institutional investors and private owners alike.

Our leadership brings together certified structural engineers, veteran construction project managers (PMP), safety auditors (OSHA), and Virtual Design Construction (VDC/BIM) specialists who collaborate seamlessly under one unified standard of excellence.

OUR METHODOLOGY

The 6-Stage Construction Lifecycle

A disciplined, predictable framework designed to transition your project from initial vision to certified asset completion without friction.

01

Consultation & Feasibility

Thorough review of architectural briefs, client expectations, land topography, soil strata reports, and preliminary statutory zoning allowances.

02

Planning & Pre-construction

Detailed Bill of Quantities (BOQ), itemized work breakdown schedules (WBS), procurement logistics, and subcontractor vetting for cost protection.

03

Design & BIM Coordination

Synchronizing architectural, structural, and MEP designs using 3D BIM models to eliminate spatial clashes before pouring foundation concrete.

04

Precision Site Execution

Mobilization of machinery, daily site supervision by resident civil engineers, automated material tracking, and rigorous schedule adherence.

05

Quality Inspection & Testing

Comprehensive slump testing, cube crushing, hydro-testing of plumbing systems, and electrical load balance audits by independent certifiers.

06

Handover & Post-Occupancy

Delivery of comprehensive as-built drawings, operations manuals, occupancy certificates, and warranty-backed post-handover support.

ABOUT ARTEEVA

Plan the Work, Measure It, and Report It Clearly

Arteeva has two divisions sharing one discipline: plan the work, measure it, and report it clearly. Arteeva Consulting provides project management consultancy centred on planning, scheduling, cost management, coordination and project controls. We help clients set realistic baselines for time and cost, keep design and procurement deliverables moving, and understand where a project stands and why.

Arteeva Construction executes select small-scale construction works, built with our own site team and specialist subcontractors, and managed to the same planning, cost and progress discipline. We work alongside clients' design teams, suppliers and contractors, running the two divisions separately so that our consulting advice stays independent.

  • Arteeva Consulting: Schedules, budgets, design and procurement coordination, progress and cost reporting.
  • Independent Advice: We do not act as contractor on projects where we provide project management consultancy.
  • Arteeva Construction: Select small-scale works executed to an agreed scope, programme and budget in writing.

HOW WE WORK

Principles That Guide Our Consulting & Execution

Our approach is technical and practical: logic-based planning, consistent measurement, transparent reporting, and strict division independence.

Integrated Management

Time, cost, design and procurement progress are managed together as one coordinated system, not in separate disconnected reports.

Transparent Derivation

You can see clearly how every schedule date, cost forecast, and variance number was derived, with full baseline logic and assumptions.

Measurable Progress

Progress and cost are tracked consistently against defined physical and financial measures rather than subjective site impressions.

Practical Reporting

Dashboards, lookahead schedules, and monthly variance reports are short, readable, and aimed directly at timely management decisions.

Two Divisions, Kept Separate

We do not act as contractor on projects where we provide project management consultancy, keeping our consulting advice completely independent.

Built to a Written Plan

For construction works, we take on a limited number of projects at a time, tracking each against an agreed scope, programme, and budget.

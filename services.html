Consulting & Construction Services

Independent project management consultancy for planning, cost, coordination and control, alongside select small-scale construction works.

TWO SEPARATE DIVISIONS

Arteeva Consulting & Arteeva Construction

Arteeva Consulting provides the planning, cost, coordination and control disciplines that keep a project on track from brief to handover, working alongside your design team, suppliers and contractors. Arteeva Construction executes select small-scale construction works with our own site team and specialist subcontractors.

Arteeva Consulting 01

Planning and Scheduling

We help clients set realistic baselines for time and establish clear schedule logic from brief to handover. Our planning service covers work breakdown, baseline schedule development, logic and critical path definition, and lookahead planning—delivering a baseline schedule and programme narrative that sets out the plan, its logic, and the assumptions behind it.

Work Breakdown Structure Baseline Schedule & Narrative Logic & Critical Path Lookahead Planning
Discuss Planning & Scheduling
Arteeva Consulting 02

Cost Management and Control

Maintain clear control of project cost and budget at every stage. We provide budget setup support, ongoing cost tracking, cash-flow forecasting, variance reporting, and change and variation tracking. You receive a clear project budget, cash-flow forecast showing what the project is expected to cost and when, and a structured change and variation log.

Budget Setup Support Cost Tracking & Cash-Flow Forecast Cost Variance Reporting Change & Variation Log
Discuss Cost Management
Arteeva Consulting 03

Design and Procurement Coordination

We facilitate and coordinate design and procurement deliverables between the client, design team, suppliers and site: drawing and submittal tracking, RFI and approval follow-up, material approval tracking, procurement schedules and long-lead item monitoring. Decisions and deliverables arrive when the programme needs them.

Drawing & Submittal Tracking RFI & Approval Follow-Up Material Approval Tracking Long-Lead Item Monitoring
Discuss Design & Procurement Coordination
Arteeva Consulting 04

Project Controls, Reporting & Risk Monitoring

Understand where your project stands against plan and why. We provide progress monitoring and schedule updates, earned value where it suits the project, dashboards and management reports, alongside risk and schedule monitoring for early identification of slippage, cost pressure and critical-path constraints.

Progress Monitoring & Updates Earned Value & Dashboards Monthly Variance Reports Early Slippage & Risk Warning
Discuss Project Controls & Reporting
Arteeva Consulting 05

Delay, Time and Change Analysis

When projects encounter disruption or scope changes, we carry out technical critical path and time impact analysis. We deliver a clear delay or time impact analysis report establishing the cause of delay and its effect on completion, providing the technical schedule basis for Extension of Time (EOT) submissions where appropriate.

Critical Path Analysis Time Impact Analysis Report EOT Technical Schedule Basis Change Effect on Time & Cost
Discuss Delay & Time Analysis
Arteeva Consulting 06

Project Management Support

Hands-on coordination between project parties to keep day-to-day execution aligned with your baseline plan. Our support encompasses project governance, structured meeting follow-up, decision and action tracking, and clear stakeholder reporting across your design team, suppliers and contractors.

Multi-Party Coordination Governance & Meeting Follow-Up Decision & Action Tracking Stakeholder Reporting
Discuss Project Management Support
Arteeva Construction 07

Residential, Commercial & Industrial Buildings

Arteeva Construction executes select small-scale construction works, including new residential and commercial buildings, warehouses, sheds and industrial buildings. Every project starts with an agreed scope, programme and budget in writing, and is tracked against them through to handover, with changes recorded and their effect on cost and time agreed.

Residential & Commercial Buildings Warehouses & Industrial Buildings Built to an Agreed Written Plan Recorded Change & Time Control

Arteeva Construction 08

Civil Works, Renovation & Fit-Out

Site development, civil works, and the renovation, refurbishment and interior fit-out of existing spaces. Our site team and labour carry out the core work, while specialist trades are brought in as subcontractors managed by our site team—supported by stage quality checks, agreed site safety arrangements, and organised handover documentation.

HOW WE WORK

What You Receive & Our Independence

Integrated, transparent, measurable, and practical delivery—with our consultancy and construction divisions kept strictly separate.

From Arteeva Consulting

Baseline schedule and narrative, budget and cash-flow forecast, design and procurement tracker, monthly variance reports, change logs, delay analysis, and lookahead dashboards.

Two Divisions, Kept Separate

We do not act as contractor on projects where we provide project management consultancy, and our consultancy advice is not tied to our construction division.

From Arteeva Construction

Completed buildings, warehouse, civil, and fit-out works built to the agreed scope, with recorded stage quality checks, a safely managed site, and handover documentation.

ABOUT ARTEEVA

Plan the Work, Measure It, and Report It Clearly

Arteeva has two divisions of equal focus, sharing one discipline: plan the work, measure it, and report it clearly. Arteeva Consulting provides project management consultancy. Its work centres on planning, scheduling, cost management, coordination and project controls. We help clients set realistic baselines for time and cost, keep design and procurement deliverables moving, and understand where a project stands and why.

Arteeva Construction executes select small-scale construction works, built with our own site team and specialist subcontractors, and managed to the same planning, cost and progress discipline. We keep this division deliberately small and take on a limited number of works at a time, so each project receives close management attention.

We work alongside clients' design teams, suppliers and contractors, and we run the two divisions separately so that our consulting advice stays independent. Our approach is technical and practical: logic-based planning, consistent measurement and transparent reporting.

Arteeva Consulting

  • Stage quality checks, agreed site safety arrangements, and handover documentation
HOW WE WORK

Principles of Our Practice

How we manage time, cost, coordination, and site execution while keeping our consulting and construction divisions strictly separate.

Integrated

Time, cost, design and procurement progress are managed together as a connected discipline, not in separate isolated reports.

Transparent

You can see how every schedule date and cost number was derived, backed by a clear programme narrative, logic, and stated assumptions.

Measurable

Progress and cost are tracked consistently against defined baselines and earned value measures where appropriate, not general impressions.

Practical

Management reports, lookahead schedules, and project dashboards are short, readable, and aimed directly at supporting timely project decisions.

Independent

Arteeva keeps its consultancy and construction work separate. If any other arrangement is ever proposed, it is disclosed to the client in writing before work begins.

Built to a Plan

Every construction work starts with an agreed scope, programme and budget in writing, and changes are recorded with their effect on cost and time agreed.

Deliverables Across the Project Lifecycle

Concrete outputs delivered by Arteeva Consulting to control your project, alongside the execution discipline of Arteeva Construction.

Baseline Schedule & Narrative

02

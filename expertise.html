OUR DOMAINS

Core Engineering & Management Disciplines

Detailed technical competency frameworks maintained across all ARTEEVA jobsites.

Construction Planning

Developing critical path method (CPM) master schedules utilizing Oracle Primavera P6 and MS Project. We isolate potential material bottleneck sequences, model weather contingency buffers, and coordinate multi-contractor handover milestones.

  • CPM Schedule Baseline Generation
  • Resource Histogram Leveling
  • Supply Chain Lead-Time Mapping

Structural Engineering

Comprehensive high-load reinforced concrete and structural steel frame calculations. Our structural engineers analyze wind shear pressures, lateral seismic load responses, soil-structure interactions, and dynamic vibration damping.

  • Seismic Zone IV / V Reinforcement
  • Post-Tensioned (PT) Flat Slabs
  • Deep Bored Foundation Piles

Project Management

Institutional project governance balancing cost, schedule, and quality baselines. We deploy certified Project Management Professionals (PMP) to oversee daily site progress, subcontractor bill validation, and statutory compliance.

  • Earned Value Management (EVM)
  • Transparent Change Order Review
  • Real-Time Digital Daily Site Logs

Quality Assurance

Operating under ISO 9001 certified Quality Management Systems. Our QA/QC inspectors maintain rigorous testing regimens at independent certified laboratories, ensuring zero deviation from architectural specifications.

  • Concrete Compression Cube Testing
  • Rebar Tensile & Bend Verifications
  • Ultrasonic Non-Destructive Testing

Safety Management

A proactive, zero-harm health and safety standard compliant with OSHA and national safety statutes. Dedicated Safety Officers conduct daily hazard analysis, scaffold load tags, fall protection audits, and emergency drill procedures.

  • Mandatory 100% PPE Enforcement
  • Daily Pre-Shift Toolbox Briefings
  • Zero-Tolerance Electrical Safety

Cost Management

Disciplined quantity surveying, market intelligence, and value-engineering analysis. We ensure project owners achieve the highest structural return without overpaying for inefficient material specifications.

  • Itemized Bill of Quantities (BOQ)
  • Value Engineering Alternatives
  • Direct Mill Procurement Savings

Construction Supervision

Full-time on-site resident engineers and clerk of works ensuring that execution strictly matches design intent. From checking rebar cover blocks to verifying laser formwork alignment, our supervisors safeguard structural accuracy.

  • Laser Alignment & Plumb Checks
  • Pre-Pour Rebar Inspection Sign-Off
  • Embedded MEP Sleeve Coordination

Sustainable Construction

Eco-conscious building engineering supporting LEED, IGBC, and GRIHA certifications. We integrate low-carbon concrete mixes, construction waste segregation, rainwater harvesting percolation tanks, and solar photovoltaic mounts.

  • Fly-Ash & GGBS Supplementary Mixes
  • On-Site Greywater Recycling
  • Construction Demolition Recycling

Modern Building Practices

Adopting industrial prefabrication, pre-cast concrete columns and slabs, aluminum modular formwork systems (MIVAN), and pre-engineered steel buildings (PEB) to dramatically compress site delivery timelines.

  • Aluminum Monolithic Formwork
  • Modular Pre-Cast Structural Units
  • 3D Drone Topographical Scanning

HOW TO REPLACE IMAGES WITH YOUR OWN ASSETS:
-------------------------------------------
1. Place your optimized JPG or WebP images inside this `images/` directory (e.g. `hero-bg.jpg`, `project-skyline.jpg`).
2. Update the `src` attribute in the respective HTML files (`index.html`, `about.html`, etc.) from the Unsplash URL to your local relative path:
   `src="images/hero-bg.jpg"`
3. Always maintain descriptive `alt` tags and keep `loading="lazy"` on images below the fold for optimal SEO and Core Web Vitals performance.
